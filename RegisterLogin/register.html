Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2025

We are committed to protecting your personal information and providing a secure platform for managing book borrowings. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

1. What Information We Collect
  • Full name, university ID (e.g., UWU/ICT/22/100)
  • Email address, age, gender, address
  • Contact numbers and encrypted passwords
  • Book details, borrow/return status, availability
2. How We Use Your Information
  • To manage borrowing and returning of books
  • To calculate fines for late returns
  • To notify you when reserved books become available
  • To generate fine reports and history
3. Borrowing & Reservation Rules
  • Maximum of 2 books per student (borrowed or reserved)
  • Books must be returned within 14 days
  • Reservations are available for unavailable books
4. Fine Calculation Policy

If you return a book late, a fine will be applied as follows:

Delay Fine
1–7 days late Rs. 5 per day
8–28 days late Rs. 10 per week
29+ days late Rs. 50 per month
Example: 50 days late = 1 month + 2 weeks + 6 days → Rs. 100
5. How We Protect Your Data
  • Passwords are encrypted using secure hashing
  • All data stored in a secure MySQL database
  • Only authorized staff have access to records
6. Your Rights
  • You can edit your profile anytime
  • You can cancel reservations before pickup
  • You can return books anytime (subject to fines)
7. Data Sharing

Your data is never shared with external parties. All data is handled within the university's internal system only.
